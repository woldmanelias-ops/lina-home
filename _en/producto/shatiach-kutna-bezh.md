<p>A flat-woven rug in natural cotton, in a warm sand tone that leans beige rather than grey. That tone works particularly well in Israeli living rooms, where direct light is strong — rugs that are too pale flatten out in that light, and dark ones shrink the room.</p>

<p>The weave feels soft underfoot without sinking, so it holds up in walkways and not only under a sofa. Cotton breathes, which matters in summer: synthetic rugs trap heat, and you feel it in a room without air conditioning.</p>

<p>To keep it: vacuum once a week. Blot spots with a damp cloth and skip strong cleaners, which lighten the colour. Dry clean once a year if you want it refreshed.</p>

<p>The 160×230 size suits a living room where the sofa sits with its two front legs on the rug — the most common layout in three and four room apartments.</p>
