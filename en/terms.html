4. Shipping

Shipping is a flat ₪{{ M.envio_costo }} per order, anywhere in Israel.

5. Cancellations and returns

Under the Consumer Protection Law, 1981, you may cancel within 14 days of receiving the product or the document with the order details, whichever is later.

Send your cancellation on WhatsApp ({{ M.whatsapp_display }}) or by email ({{ M.email }}) with your order number.

Products must be returned unused and in their original packaging. Returning the product is the customer's responsibility{% if M.direccion and M.direccion != "" %}, to: {{ M.direccion }}{% endif %}.

For cancellations not caused by a defect, a cancellation fee of 5% of the price or ₪100, whichever is lower, applies. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the cancellation notice.

Senior citizens (65+), new immigrants and people with disabilities may cancel within 4 months, under the conditions set by law.

6. Defective or wrong items

If an item arrives defective or different from what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp with a photo. No cancellation fee applies: we collect the item at our cost and replace it or refund you in full, as you prefer.
