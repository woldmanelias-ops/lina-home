Accessibility statement

Last updated: 1 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to browse and order comfortably. We are working to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA and the Israeli Standard 5568.

What we have done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, and every page has a "skip to content" link.
  • The element in focus is clearly outlined.
  • Pages use a clear heading structure, and images have alternative text.
  • The site supports Hebrew (right to left) and English (left to right).
  • We kept a clear contrast between text and background.
  • Form fields have labels, and the cart window closes with the Esc key.
  • The site respects your system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • The site adapts to phones and screens of any size.

Payment page

The payment page is run by Tranzila, a third party. We have turned on the accessibility button they provide.

Known limitations

Some parts of the site may not be fully accessible yet, and we keep improving. If you run into a problem, please tell us and we will fix it as soon as we can.
