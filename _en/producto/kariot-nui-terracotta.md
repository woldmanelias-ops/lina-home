<p>A cushion with a pronounced ribbed texture in a warm clay tone — not orange, not pink, somewhere in between. The texture is what makes it: smooth cushions disappear into a sofa, while a textured one still reads from across the room.</p>

<p>The cover comes off with a hidden zip and washes at 30 degrees. That is worth checking elsewhere — plenty of decorative cushions are sold sewn shut, and then one stain ends the cushion.</p>

<p>The tone sits well with beige, sand and deep brown, and less well with cool grey. If your sofa is grey, two terracotta cushions alongside two in sand soften the transition.</p>

<p>45×45 is the standard decorative cushion size in Israel, so replacement inserts are easy to find anywhere.</p>
